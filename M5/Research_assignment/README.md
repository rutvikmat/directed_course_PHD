# Module 5 – Research Assignment Package

This package contains the short doctoral research proposal and supporting submission/reproducibility documents.

## Main document
- `proposal/Research_Proposal.md` – complete proposal in Markdown.
- `proposal/Research_Proposal.docx` – editable Word version.

## Supporting documents
- `supporting_docs/Submission_Checklist.md` – final checks before submission.
- `supporting_docs/Research_Questions_and_Hypotheses.md` – research questions and testable hypotheses.
- `reproducibility/Experiment_Log_Template.csv` – experiment tracking template.
- `reproducibility/README.md` – reproducibility and execution guidance.
- `references/Reference_Verification.md` – bibliography verification notes.

## Important academic note
This is a proposal, not evidence that the proposed research has been completed. The proposed PFE-HR name denotes an integrated framework concept, not a proven novel algorithm. Verify bibliographic metadata and establish novelty through a systematic literature review before formal doctoral submission. Do not claim differential privacy unless a valid mechanism and privacy accountant support the stated epsilon/delta guarantee.
