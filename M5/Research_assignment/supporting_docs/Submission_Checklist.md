# Submission checklist

- [ ] Confirm proposal title with supervisor.
- [ ] Refine target outcome, cohort and prediction horizon.
- [ ] Complete a current literature review and substantiate the research gap.
- [ ] Verify every reference's bibliographic details and DOI/URL.
- [ ] Confirm dataset licence, access conditions and ethics requirements.
- [ ] Distinguish completed prototype work from proposed future experiments.
- [ ] Ensure privacy claims are supported by a defined mechanism and accountant.
- [ ] Add institution-required cover page, student details, supervisor and formatting.
- [ ] Proofread and check citation style.
